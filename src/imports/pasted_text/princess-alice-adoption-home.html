Environment & Living Conditions

The Princess Alice Adoption Home has served the community for 96 years. The facility includes bedrooms, kitchens, and outdoor spaces designed for care and comfort. However, years of limited maintenance have taken a toll.

Key Challenges

🎨
Peeling Paint

Deteriorating walls throughout create an unwelcoming environment.

🌧️
Roof Leaks → Mould

Water ingress has led to mould growth, posing health risks.

🌿
Overgrown Garden

Outdoor spaces are unusable, limiting children's play access.

🪟
Missing Curtains

Lack of window coverings affects privacy and sleep quality.

📦

Available Resources

What's Available

  • On-site nursing care (Sister Thembi)
  • 2 transport vehicles for emergencies
  • Outdoor equipment (currently unusable)
  • Committed caregiving team

What's Missing

  • No formal education programmes
  • Limited hygiene products
  • Insufficient maintenance budget
  • No developmental stimulation tools

Key Needs & Their Impact

Poor Physical Environment High Priority

Peeling paint, roof leaks affect children's health directly.

Mould & Health Risks High Priority

Respiratory and immune risks for vulnerable infants.

Lack of Developmental Stimulation Medium-High

No formal programmes limit cognitive and social growth.
